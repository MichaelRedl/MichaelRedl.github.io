02 / Repeating sections

One form.
As many rows as you need.

Order items, project tasks or inspection points: let people add related entries right inside the form, instead of squeezing everything into a single text field.

  • Add, edit and remove rows
  • Keep related entries with the list item
  • Validate repeating data with the main form

Useful for orders, participant lists and checklists.

03 / Form rules

Forms that respond
to what people enter.

Show the right fields at the right time. Use conditions to adapt visibility, required fields, values, colours and read-only states as the form changes.

  • Combine conditions with AND / OR
  • Use field values or the signed-in user
  • Apply multiple actions in a single rule

For example: make a field required when a particular status is selected.

04 / PDF export

From form to PDF.
In your browser.

Keep a shareable record of the form you are looking at. Export its displayed values, layout, colours and repeating rows as an A4 PDF.

  • Capture the current form, including unsaved input
  • Include your layout and repeating rows
  • Generate locally, without an external PDF converter

Useful for project records, requests and inspection documentation.

Questions, answered

A few things worth knowing.

Is Jelly Forms really free?+

Yes. Jelly Forms is a free app, including the visual designer, repeating sections, form rules and PDF export. You still need Microsoft 365 / SharePoint access; those services have their own costs.

Where can I get Jelly Forms?+

The Microsoft SharePoint store release is being prepared. The app is not listed yet. A direct link will be added here when it is published.

Jelly Forms is available free in the Microsoft SharePoint store. Use the link in the getting-started section to open the listing.

Which SharePoint forms can I customise?+

Jelly Forms customises the New, Edit and View forms of modern generic SharePoint lists. Document libraries are not part of its intended scope.

Who can design forms?+

The designer requires the Manage Lists permission on the target list. Publishing also requires permission to write the form configuration. Normal form use follows each user’s SharePoint permissions.

Does PDF export save my changes?+

No. The export captures the values currently displayed, including unsaved input. Save the form separately to update the SharePoint list item.

Does Jelly Forms translate my own labels?+

The product controls are available in English and German. Your custom field labels, choices and authored content remain under your control and are not translated automatically.
